Sunday Morning Trail Run - Hazeldean Valley Trails

5KM | 10KM | 15KM

Water Points

Group Start 7h30

MEDIC on duty

Finishers Token

Walkers & Hikers Welcome

The Sunday Morning Trail Run features 3 pre-set distance options with regular water points, making it easy to focus on your training/exercise/enjoyment, without worrying about carrying extra weight or how you'll hydrate en-route.

Participation is self-timed via STRAVA and there's a weekly leaderboard for some friendly competition.

Hazeldean Valley Trails offers an excellent trail running experience suitable for new and experienced trail runners, walkers and hikers.
